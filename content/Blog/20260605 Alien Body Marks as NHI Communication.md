Like Vallee, I wonder about abduction marks. If they are made by interstellar doctors or magical spirits, why would they leave scars? 

These marks are often discovered upon waking, associated with powerful and strange experiences similar to the hypnagogic encounter phenomenon that is reported by experiencers to feel ultra-real.

If these encounters happen in a real realm outside of our space time that is accessed through dreaming, then perhaps these marks are psycho-somatic records of the communication that happens in those realms.
