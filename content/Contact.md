# FAQ
## Why I do lengthy, free, no-obligation conversations
If  you are interested in doing dreamwork with me or sharing your story with me for research, I would love to talk with you.  I offer free and no-obligation conversations for us to just chat about working together and to share stories. It is one of my favorite parts of DSETI.

I offer lengthy initial conversations because you probably have a story that you're digesting, either memories or intuitions of dreams and encounters, or else an interest in these topics in general.  Many people haven't had a chance to really process their stories with another person who knows what you're talking about and won't judge you.

It needs to be free and no-obligation because we're building rapport.  You need to trust me with your story, which defines your reality at some level to your dreaming mind.  

## Why I offer sliding-scale donation services?
My services are offered by sliding-scale donations, based on a rate of $90 USD per 90 minute session, which I will "discount" in packages or recurrent scheduling ($60 per session when recurrent).  I will send you a donation link after the session, along with links to our recording (if desired).  This process is modeled after my first mentor in Core Shamanism, Tereza Morena.

My work involves dream shamanism, which involves respecting the dream and its characters as if they are real, while learning to discern the reality and meaning of a variety of dreams.  My dream guides insist that I offer this service in this way so that cash is not a barrier.

I generally commit to an initial series of sessions for any donation level, although I may ask for permission to publish future writing on our sessions or inquiry together, which I understand as the spiritual practice of testifying.  
# How will your session or communications be used?
I will record your session only after you agree.  I will publish your story or my research on it only after you agree.  Generally, I will write a detailed report about my inquiry that I will have you review, then will publish via my blog or a document, after which I will refer to that document in my future research. 

## What type of service or research does DSETI offer?
My dreamwork services are based in Depth Hypnosis, Beyond Quantum Healing, and a variety of dreamwork training.  None of my services or trainings are clinical, therapeutic, or otherwise medical or mental health oriented, although working with dreams has therapeutic effects and is stereotypically associated with therapy.  I look to the International Association of the Study of Dreams and the Professional Association of Depth Hypnotists for ethical guidelines.

I work with alien abduction and ET/NHI/UFO/UAP contact narratives, offering shamanic dreamwork as an alternative to regression hypnosis, with the understanding that many abduction narratives, and derivative UAP disclosure and NHI contact narratives, involve powerful dreams. I work with them in a psycho-spiritual way and not as forensic hypnotherapy.

While I pursued research in these topics in a PhD program at the California Institute for Human Scienced, I withdrew for ethical concerns regarding false memories and authoritative (accredited) research into NHI/UAP encounters.  I now consider myself an independent integral researcher, which I see as a spiritual practice based on mystical interpretations of the Platonic and Western Esoteric traditions.

I am a fine and liberal artist, educated by psychologists brave enough to consider psychedics and Eastern religion, trying my best to understand my own dreams that I once thought were aliens while practicing my spiritual vocation as a Dreamer.
## Why I sometimes become friends and collaborators with my clients?
I am not offering therapy and will not work with mentally unstable or ill people.  Generally, the people I work with are on a journey.  This space is a spiritual space, dealing with what I believe to be souls.  I often observe synchronicities, omens, or dreams about my client work.  At some level, sharing a dream is a non-dual and spiritually potent act that might bind us together for the rest of our lives in some way.  

If you are really stirred up about a memory, intuition, or dream, then I will often hold space for that dream before showing up as a social person in our sessions.  Usually, that is after the initial series of three session.
