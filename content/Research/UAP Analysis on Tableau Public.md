Part of the _[Paradoxical Compass](https://legacy.dseti.org/the-paradoxical-compass/)_ analysis technique involves the visualization of pulsations of light from UFO/UAPs. The process analyzes several types of brightness or intensity of the UAP over time from video footage. I use Tableau Public to visualize the data as a way of keeping public records and because it is very easy to print 8×11.5 pages on which to perform ruler and compass geometric analysis of the pulsation.

Check out the D-SETI Tableau Public dashboard here: [https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/dseti](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/dseti)
