Wargo emphasizes that some precognitive dreams transmit emotional states rather than specific images. The dreamer may awaken overwhelmed with dread, joy, sadness, or longing without understanding why. Later, a waking event produces the same emotional intensity, creating a clear resonance between the dream’s feeling-tone and the future moment. The imagery in the dream may be symbolic or irrelevant. This supports Wargo’s thesis that emotion functions as the carrier wave of precognitive information.
