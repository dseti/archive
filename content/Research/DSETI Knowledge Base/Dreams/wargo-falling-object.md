Wargo documents a case where a dreamer sees an object falling in slow motion, accompanied by a shock-like emotional jolt. In the dream, the fall feels important though its meaning is obscure. The emotional signature is strong and disorienting. Later that day, an object falls in waking life and causes a minor injury to the dreamer or a companion. The dream’s emotional tone precisely matches the waking moment. The synchronicity feels uncanny and orchestrated. Wargo explains this as emotional resonance across time, with the dreamer’s unconscious accessing the future emotional impact.
