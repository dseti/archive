Marden describes a case in which an experiencer finds themselves gently lowered back into bed after an encounter. The descent feels soft and guided, with a soothing emotional afterglow. The transition is seamless, lacking narrative continuity. The experiencer feels unusually calm and reflective, as if returning from a symbolic dream journey. The room appears unchanged, yet emotionally charged. Marden notes that this peaceful return contrasts with the fear that often characterizes earlier phases of contact.
