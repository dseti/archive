Turner reports a strange nocturnal episode in which she suddenly feels an overwhelming compulsion to leave a room and go outside. The experience is framed as dreamlike, occurring around 3 A.M. during a period of heightened fear following Casey’s regression. The urge arises abruptly, with Turner pacing and insisting she must step outside despite the darkness and the earlier terror. She cannot explain what she expects to find.
