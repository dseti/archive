Marden describes an experiencer who feels their body become weightless during the night. They rise from the bed and float toward the ceiling. The sensation is vivid yet dreamlike, creating emotional confusion. They observe their sleeping partner from above, sensing no physical effort in the movement. A soft glow surrounds them, guiding the ascent. The event carries strong symbolic resonance. Marden presents this as a common altered-state component of ERT cases that blurs waking and dream boundaries.
