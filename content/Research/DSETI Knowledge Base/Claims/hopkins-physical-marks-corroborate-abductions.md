This claim asserts that anomalous body marks—scoop marks, triangle scars, burns, or geometric impressions—corroborate abduction events described under hypnosis or spontaneous recall. Hopkins argues that these marks appear suddenly and match patterns seen across unrelated witnesses. Evidence includes photographs, medical examinations, and repeated descriptions of marks discovered after missing time episodes. He interprets these scars as physical artifacts of medical or technological procedures performed by nonhuman entities. Conceptually, DSETI evaluates this claim as Weak-to-Moderate, noting the marks are real but ambiguous and easily overinterpreted.
