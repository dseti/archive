This claim asserts that entities often use screen images—such as owls, deer, or humans—to mask their true appearance during encounters. Hopkins argues that these images function as psychic camouflage, enabling abductors to approach individuals without triggering full awareness. Evidence includes multiple witnesses recalling animal or human figures that later unfold into nonhuman entities under regression. Conceptually, DSETI evaluates the claim as Moderate symbolically but Weak literally, emphasizing symbolic dream-mask dynamics.
