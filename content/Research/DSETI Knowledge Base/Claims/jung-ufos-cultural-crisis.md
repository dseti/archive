This claim states that mass UFO sightings tend to cluster during times of cultural tension and uncertainty. Jung argues that such periods activate archetypal material, which then manifests as collective visions, rumors, or symbolic images projected onto ambiguous sky phenomena. These waves express underlying social and psychological stress.
