Detecting Meaning in Dream Reports by Kelly Bulkeley presents linguistic and thematic analysis methods for interpreting dream content. Bulkeley integrates qualitative and quantitative approaches to exploring recurring themes, emotions, and symbols. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan cites Bulkeley as a methodological foundation for dream-content analysis in the Dream Archive. Bulkeley’s models guide entity, emotion, and motif extraction in DSETI. For DSETI, this work provides essential tools for analyzing experiencer dreams systematically and consistently.
