Dreams and Divine Inspiration by Linda Abraham explores ancient and medieval traditions in which dreams were considered communications from gods, angels, or cosmic beings. The text provides cross-cultural examples of dream-based instruction and revelation. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan cites Abraham while comparing ancient visionary encounters with modern entity dreams. It supports interpreting contact dreams within a historical lineage of imaginal communication. For DSETI, this work contextualizes NHI dream encounters as part of a long tradition of dream-based guidance and symbolic revelation.
