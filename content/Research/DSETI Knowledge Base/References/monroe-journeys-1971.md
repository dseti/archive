Journeys Out of the Body by Robert Monroe documents Monroe’s spontaneous out-of-body experiences and the techniques he developed to navigate nonphysical realities. His work inspired decades of research into OBEs and altered states. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan cites Monroe while comparing abduction imagery to OBE-like states. Monroe’s descriptions of energetic environments and entity encounters parallel many dream-contact reports. For DSETI, Monroe offers a vocabulary and phenomenological map for nonordinary travel, lucidity, and communication in subtle states.
