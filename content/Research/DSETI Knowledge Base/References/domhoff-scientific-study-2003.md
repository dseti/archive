The Scientific Study of Dreams by G. William Domhoff presents a comprehensive overview of cognitive neuroscience, dream content analysis, and large-scale database approaches. Domhoff argues that dreams reflect waking concerns and psychological dispositions. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan uses Domhoff’s methods in designing the Dream Archive’s analytic system. Domhoff’s approach supports systematic comparison across experiencer dream datasets. For DSETI, the work provides empirical grounding for analyzing dream narratives as coherent psychological expressions that may also include anomalous features.
