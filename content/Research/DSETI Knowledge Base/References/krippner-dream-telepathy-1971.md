Dream Telepathy by Ullman and Krippner reports a decade of laboratory studies testing dream-mediated telepathy at Maimonides Hospital. Results suggested significant correspondence between target images and dream content in controlled conditions. DSETI tracks this reference because Wargo, Rekshan, and the wider dream-ESP literature cite these experiments as the strongest empirical support for dream-based psi. The work is central to evaluating dream-mediated contact hypotheses. For DSETI, Dream Telepathy provides methodological precedents for assessing whether dream imagery contains nonlocal informational content relevant to NHI communication.
