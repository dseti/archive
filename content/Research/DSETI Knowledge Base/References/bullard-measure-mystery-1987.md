UFO Abductions: The Measure of a Mystery by Thomas E. Bullard organizes and analyzes hundreds of abduction narratives from around the world. Bullard identifies structural stages, recurring motifs, and cross-cultural consistencies, establishing the first scholarly typology of abduction phenomena. DSETI tracks this reference because it is cited in Ring, Rekshan, and Mack as a core analytic foundation for abduction research. Bullard’s structured approach informs both phenomenological and content-analysis methods in DSETI. For DSETI, Bullard’s work is essential for comparing dream encounters with classical abduction structure. It supports evaluating whether dream motifs reflect cultural templates or deeper psychospiritual patterns.
