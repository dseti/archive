Hypnagogia by Andreas Mavromatis is a comprehensive study of the transitional state between wakefulness and sleep. It surveys hallucinations, voices, visions, and other experiences that often resemble abduction, contact, or symbolic dream imagery. DSETI tracks this reference because Wargo cites Mavromatis when discussing the neurophenomenology of dream states, and Rekshan references it when analyzing abduction imagery as dreamlike. The book is a crossroad between psychology, psi research, and visionary states. For DSETI, Hypnagogia provides essential background on the liminal states where many contact experiences originate. It supports interpreting abductions as dreamlike states with symbolic or telepathic qualities.
