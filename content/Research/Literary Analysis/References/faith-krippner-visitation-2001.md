The study identifies frequencies of alien, spirit, and OBE-like encounters. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan uses Faith and Krippner’s statistics to estimate prevalence of entity encounters in the general population. It is critical for calculating expected rates of dream contact imagery. For DSETI, this study anchors experiencer dream reports within an empirical dream-research baseline.
