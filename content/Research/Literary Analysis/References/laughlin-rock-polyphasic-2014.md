Polyphasic Cultures and Consciousness by Charles Laughlin and Adam Rock contrasts monophasic cultures, which marginalize dreams and altered states, with polyphasic cultures that embrace them. The book surveys anthropological evidence for culturally supported visionary cognition. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan cites Laughlin and Rock when comparing Western skepticism about dreams with cultures that consider dreams as legitimate sources of knowledge. It provides a framework for reinterpreting dream contact experiences. For DSETI, the polyphasic model helps elevate dream states as valid experiential domains for exploring NHI contact and inspiration.
