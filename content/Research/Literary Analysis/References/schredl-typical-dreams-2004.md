Typical Dreams by Michael Schredl analyzes common dream themes across large surveys. Schredl reports prevalence rates for themes such as falling, being chased, and UFO/alien encounters. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan cites Schredl’s data to estimate population-level prevalence of alien/UFO dream motifs. The study helps differentiate common dream imagery from anomalous patterns. For DSETI, Typical Dreams provides baseline statistical context necessary for interpreting experiencer dream frequency.
