Supernormal by Dean Radin investigates yogic siddhis—classical Indian descriptions of extraordinary abilities—and compares them to modern psi research. Radin argues that ancient descriptions of precognition, telepathy, and clairvoyance align with contemporary experimental data. DSETI tracks this reference because Wargo cites Radin’s work in Time Loops while discussing cross-cultural interpretations of precognitive and telepathic experiences. The book strengthens connections between mystical traditions and psi research. For DSETI, Supernormal supplies conceptual parallels for understanding entity encounters, symbolic dreams, and intuitive knowledge as part of a broader psi spectrum.
