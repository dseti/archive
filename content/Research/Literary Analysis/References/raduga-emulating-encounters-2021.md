Emulating Alien and UFO Encounters in REM Sleep by Michael Raduga and colleagues reports experiments inducing sleep paralysis and hypnagogic hallucinations to reproduce abduction-like encounters. Results show vivid entity encounters are easily triggered through REM intrusion. DSETI tracks this reference because Rekshan uses Raduga’s findings to contextualize the dreamlike qualities of many experiencer reports. It supports the hypothesis that sleep states may simulate or host contact-like experiences. For DSETI, this work anchors the phenomenology of paralysis and entity imagery within a scientific framework, helping differentiate state-induced content from deeper symbolic or psi-mediated experiences.
