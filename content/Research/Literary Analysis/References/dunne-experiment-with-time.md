An Experiment with Time by J. W. Dunne presents a pioneering theory of precognitive dreaming based on Dunne’s personal dream logs and thousands of collected cases. Dunne proposes that consciousness transcends linear time and can perceive events before they occur. His theory strongly influenced later psi research. DSETI tracks this reference because Wargo repeatedly cites Dunne in Time Loops and frames him as a major precursor to modern models of retrocausation. Dunne’s insights also underlie many contemporary approaches to understanding precognitive dreams in scientific and popular contexts. For DSETI, An Experiment with Time provides a historical anchor for exploring time anomalies, dream precognition, and nonlinear consciousness. Its ideas support interpreting experiencer dreams as part of a broader temporal ecology.
