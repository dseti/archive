This claim proposes that UFO encounters often carry a numinous emotional quality similar to religious or mystical experiences. Jung observes that witnesses frequently describe awe, dread, fascination, or profound significance even when the sighting is visually ambiguous. These reactions indicate that UFOs operate as carriers of archetypal meaning rather than ordinary objects.
