Casey recounts another dream occurring after a night of intimacy. In the dream, he gets up to go to the bathroom and suddenly senses that someone is watching him. He turns toward the window, only to find it transformed into a clear opening revealing the yard. A group of blond beings stands silently outside, watching him without expression. Their attention feels invasive, and he experiences both attraction and discomfort. The dream deepens Casey’s sense that these beings take an interest in him and observe at moments of vulnerability, leaving him uneasy about the boundaries between dreams and intrusions.
