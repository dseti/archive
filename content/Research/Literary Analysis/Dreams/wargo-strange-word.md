Wargo discusses cases where a dream supplies a strange or novel word that seems meaningless at the time. The dreamer often dismisses it as random. The dream may include vivid emotional coloration around the word. Later, the exact word or phrase appears unexpectedly in conversation, media, or written form. The recognition produces a jolt of synchronicity, revealing a loop between future language exposure and past dream imagery. Wargo argues that words in precognitive dreams often represent future verbal stimuli absorbed unconsciously.
