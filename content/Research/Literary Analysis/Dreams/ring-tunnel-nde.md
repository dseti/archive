Ring includes an engineer’s account of passing through a dark tunnel while near death. He perceives a distant light and feels pulled toward it. As he moves forward, the tunnel widens and takes on dreamlike, weightless qualities. He encounters a radiant presence that communicates without words. The presence offers a choice of continuing or returning to life. The emotional impact is overwhelming and peaceful. Ring notes that this NDE tunnel structure shares symbolic similarities with abduction corridor imagery, both operating like dream pathways.
