James describes a frightening dream in which he is shoved to the ground by a group of fast-moving beings. He is unable to resist them as they immobilize him. One of the entities then pushes a long tube down his throat. He tries to escape but cannot move, describing sensations of gagging and a bitter taste. After they withdraw the tube, another being approaches and appears to intervene, causing the group to back away. The dream leaves James distressed and confused, and the following morning he wakes with a sore throat and bitter taste, raising questions about whether the event was entirely symbolic or something more literal.
