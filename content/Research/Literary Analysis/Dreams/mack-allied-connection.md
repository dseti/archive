The emotional ambivalence feels dreamlike, similar to encounters with powerful figures in symbolic dreams. Experiencers describe the beings as distant yet intimate, threatening yet protective. The relationship carries an uncanny emotional charge that does not match ordinary waking interactions. Mack interprets this as a sign that the encounter operates within a symbolic field where personal and transpersonal forces merge, producing a dream-encounter dynamic that defies simple categorization.
