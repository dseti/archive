Barney’s regression includes a scene in which he is guided down a curved corridor inside the craft. The walls feel too close, and the air seems absent of sound. He experiences disorientation, as though the environment is shifting with each step. The corridor imagery resembles classic dream symbolism: a tunnel leading deeper into unconscious material. Barney feels both resistance and compliance. He cannot explain why he follows. Fuller uses this corridor scene to illustrate the symbolic texture of the Hills’ encounter as it emerged under hypnosis.
