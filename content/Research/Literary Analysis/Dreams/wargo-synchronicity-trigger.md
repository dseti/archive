Wargo explains that dream fragments can become triggers for later synchronicity. A dream may include a small symbolic element—an animal, number, shape, or phrase. At the time, it seems trivial. Later, that exact element appears in waking life, initiating a chain of meaningful coincidences that reveal the dream’s retrocausal function. The emotional impact binds the moments together. Wargo argues that dreams prepare the psyche for future meaningful events through these symbolic triggers.
