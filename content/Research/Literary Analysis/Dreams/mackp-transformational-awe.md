Mack notes that many experiencers return from encounters with a sense of profound awe that lingers like the emotional residue of a powerful dream. They report feeling connected to cosmic questions about identity, origin, and purpose. This aftereffect does not feel like ordinary memory integration but resembles the unfolding of meaning after a significant dream. The experiencer may interpret their life differently, sensing an expansion of consciousness. Mack interprets this awe-filled afterstate as a transformative component of the encounter, aligning it with mythic and dreamlike processes that influence spiritual development.
