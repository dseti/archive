During recall work described by Marden, Denise Stoner reports a moment when she senses beings watching her from the darkness. She cannot see them clearly, yet feels their attention as a palpable presence. The scene resembles a dream where eyes exist without forms. She feels both dread and fascination, as though pulled into a symbolic confrontation. The environment seems unreal, with sound and light flattened. Marden identifies this as a dreamlike memory fragment that aligns with common pre-abduction sensory cues.
