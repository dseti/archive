A central element of Jacobs’s cases involves the abductee staring directly into the eyes of a being. One woman recalls an extended moment in which the being’s unblinking gaze causes her perception to dissolve. The scene feels unreal, like a dream where symbolic figures exert overwhelming influence. She describes the stare as pulling her inward, creating a trance state where fear and calm intermingle. The being communicates silently, and she feels compelled to comply without knowing why. Jacobs notes that the staring episodes often induce a dreamlike surrender of agency, marking the onset of deeper procedures.
